# Privacy Policy – Yair Browser

Yair Browser does not collect, transmit or sell personal data. The list of allowed sites, the list of blocked domains discovered while browsing, and the management PIN (stored as a salted hash) are stored only on your device.

Websites you open are operated by third parties and receive the usual information any website receives (such as IP address); they are governed by their own policies. Cookies set by allowed sites are kept in the app's browser storage on your device.

Subscriptions and payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.

Write Hebrew copy like an experienced Israeli copywriter with taste, not like an AI: living natural Hebrew, varied
sentence length, warm and concrete, persuasive without sounding like an ad. No AI clichés ("בואו להיות חלק",
"יחד יוצרים שינוי", "חוויה בלתי נשכחת", "הרבה מעבר ל"), no automatic triplets, rhetorical questions, staged contrasts,
"לא רק X אלא גם Y", or sentences chopped for drama. Every sentence earns its place. Final test: would a tasteful
Israeli say it this way? Show polished copy only, no explanations. Never promise unmeasured times or invented proof.

**Learned 2026-11-02 (owner: "יופי, קח את ההגיון איך לכתוב"):** rejected "גב", "רוח גבית", "שליחי מצווה" as forced
cleverness, and "מתן נותנת לכם קהילה שמגייסת" because the product doesn't literally give that. Approved: concrete
outcomes the product really delivers, in plain spoken Hebrew ("דף קמפיין שמשכנע לתרום", "מתרימים שמביאים את
החברים שלהם", "תרומות שנכנסות ישר לחשבון שלכם"). Test each line: is it literally true, and would a person say it?
He also rejected feature-description headlines as "not marketing": the headline needs a hook (his own idea that won:
"מתן נותנת לכם" + rotating concrete benefits); features go in the line under it. Hall/event night is not a headline
topic (exception approved 2026-11-04: "מסך חי לערב ההתרמה" may be ONE of the short rotating lines). Look at foreign competitors (Charidy, Givebutter, GoFundMe Pro) for patterns, never words.
**Before showing or shipping any copy** (owner: "תלמד איך לכתוב לפני שאתה מאשר"): run every line through these
rules yourself and cut what fails; never hand him a draft he has to reject for reasons already listed here.

**Why:** the owner judged earlier Matan copy "kindergarten level" (2026-10-28) and gave these rules.
